using 10.0.0.0/1 network

C = Convert it - 3500 = 12bits
S = Subract it -  /32 - /12 = /20 (3rd, 16i) 255.255.240.0
I = Ipasok sa Hiwa - Ipasok sa 2nd si 16i

concentrix.com Network

Last: 10.0.31.254/20
